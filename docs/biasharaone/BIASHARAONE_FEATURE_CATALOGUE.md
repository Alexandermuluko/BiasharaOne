# BiasharaOne Feature Catalogue

Each feature below is an independent product requirement derived from discovery evidence. It does not prescribe existing source structure.

| ID | Feature | Purpose | Roles | Workflow | Inputs | Outputs | Rules and validations | Errors | Dependencies | Acceptance |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| FC-001 | Fast sale | Ring up and complete a transaction | Cashier, manager | Search/scan items, edit lines, select customer, calculate tax, take payments, print | Items, quantities, prices, tender, customer | Sale, stock movements, payment record, receipt | Stock and credit checks; decimal totals; idempotency | Stock unavailable, invalid tender, duplicate retry | Catalogue, inventory, branch, printer | One committed sale and stock effect per submission |
| FC-002 | Held and recalled bills | Preserve incomplete sales | Cashier, manager | Hold bill, list holds, recall or cancel | Hold identity, lines | Held bill | Branch/user scope | Missing or already recalled | Sales | No stock or revenue effect until completion |
| FC-003 | Returns and refunds | Reverse approved sale effects | Cashier, manager | Find sale, select lines, approval, tender reversal | Original sale, quantity, reason | Refund, stock movement, audit | Cannot exceed sold quantity; approval based on role | Expired window, over-return | Sale, stock, payment | Exact, single reversal |
| FC-004 | Item catalogue | Maintain sellable products | Inventory, manager | Create/edit/archive item | Name, SKU, barcode, unit, prices, tax | Item record | SKU/barcode uniqueness; valid tax; archive preserves history | Duplicate, invalid unit | Category, supplier | Authorized item appears in POS |
| FC-005 | Variants | Offer product variations | Inventory, manager | Add option combinations | Options, SKU, price/stock policy | Variant record | Stable ID; no ambiguous combinations | Duplicate variant | Item catalogue | Variant selection updates line correctly |
| FC-006 | Inventory balances | Track stock by branch | Inventory, manager | View and reconcile balances | Item, branch | On-hand and available stock | No negative stock unless policy permits | Missing branch, negative result | Catalogue, branch | Balance equals approved movements |
| FC-007 | Stock adjustment | Correct inventory | Manager | Enter adjustment and reason | Item, branch, quantity, reason | Stock movement | Approval and reason required | Unauthorized, invalid quantity | Inventory | Adjustment changes balance once |
| FC-008 | Stock transfer | Move stock between branches | Manager | Create, send, receive | Source, destination, lines | Transfer events | Sufficient source stock; destination confirmation | Missing/partial transfer | Branches, inventory | Source/destination net correctly |
| FC-009 | Receiving | Record supplier goods | Inventory | Create receiving against order or direct | Supplier, lines, cost, date | Purchase, stock, cost | Supplier/branch authorization; exactly-once receiving | Duplicate invoice, over-receive | Suppliers, purchase, inventory | Stock/cost update once |
| FC-010 | Purchase orders | Authorize future purchases | Manager | Draft, approve, send, receive | Supplier, lines, terms | Purchase order | Approval state transitions | Invalid state change | Suppliers | Received quantity reconciles |
| FC-011 | Supplier returns | Return damaged goods | Manager | Select receiving/lines | Supplier, quantity, reason | Return, stock reversal | No over-return | Unauthorized | Purchasing, inventory | Stock and supplier balance reconcile |
| FC-012 | Customer | Maintain customer records | Manager, cashier | Create/edit/search customer | Identity, contact, credit terms | Customer record | Consent and privacy rules; normalized phone | Duplicate or restricted data | Branch | Authorized search and statement |
| FC-013 | Customer credit | Allow account sales | Manager | Apply terms and invoice customer | Customer, limit, due date | Credit balance, statement | Limit/overdue policy | Limit exceeded | Customer, sale | Every charge/payment recorded |
| FC-014 | Customer payment | Receive against balance | Cashier, manager | Allocate payment | Customer, amount, tender | Payment and balance | Cannot overpay unless policy permits | Duplicate submission | Credit ledger | Balance and receipt reconcile |
| FC-015 | Expenses | Record cash/outflow | Manager, accountant | Create, classify, approve | Category, amount, supplier, date | Expense | Tax/document policy | Missing approval | Branch, register | Expense appears in reports once |
| FC-016 | Cash register | Manage till float and close | Cashier, manager | Open, move cash, close | Opening/closing amounts | Register, shift | Denominations optional | Amount mismatch | Sales, expenses | Closing totals reconcile |
| FC-017 | Branch management | Configure outlets | Owner, manager | Create/configure/assign branches | Branch details, settings | Branch record | Stable branch IDs | Unauthorized | Tenant control | Cross-branch access enforced |
| FC-018 | Employee accounts | Manage workforce | Owner, manager | Invite/create/deactivate | Identity, role, branches | User membership | Unique business identity policy | Duplicate user | Auth, branch | Inactive user cannot transact |
| FC-019 | Roles and permissions | Enforce access | Owner, manager | Assign standard/custom permissions | Role, actions, branches | Role membership | Privilege escalation blocked | Unauthorized grant | Auth | Restricted action denied |
| FC-020 | Sales reports | Review trade | Manager, accountant | Filter, compare, export | Date, branch, cashier, tender | Totals/detail/export | Scope and timezone | Invalid range | Sales | Totals reconcile to source |
| FC-021 | Tax reports | Support local tax preparation | Accountant, manager | Configure/report/export | Tax rates, period | Tax summaries | Policy and official mapping verified | Unsupported tax | Tax configuration | Output reviewed before filing |
| FC-022 | Inventory reports | Identify stock status | Manager | Filter and export | Branch, category, stock state | Valuation/movement report | Valuation method documented | Inconsistent cost | Inventory | Report reconciles |
| FC-023 | Thermal receipt | Provide customer proof | Cashier | Print or reprint | Sale, template | Printed/PDF receipt | No duplicate stock/payment replay | Printer unavailable | Printer adapter | Reprint is a display/print copy only |
| FC-024 | Barcode labels | Label stock | Inventory | Search/print labels | Item/variant quantity | Labels | Valid barcode format | Printer unavailable | Printer | Printed identifiers match record |
| FC-025 | Import | Bring in authorized data | Owner, manager | Upload, map, validate, confirm | CSV/Excel, mapping | Import result | Preview; reject invalid rows; idempotency | Partial/invalid import | Data service | Valid rows complete, invalid rows report |
| FC-026 | Export | Extract business data | Owner, accountant | Choose scope and format | Date/entity/filter | Download file | Signed-in authorization; tenant scope | Unauthorized scope | Reports/data | Export contains only authorized data |
| FC-027 | Local backup | Protect local data | Owner, manager | Create/verify/restore | Destination, passphrase | Backup package | Integrity, encryption and explicit restore | Tamper, restore conflict | Local storage | Drill restores within RPO |
| FC-028 | Cloud backup | Protect cloud data | Platform operator, owner | Schedule/monitor/restore | Retention policy | Backup job, restore | Tenant isolation, audit, encryption | Job failure | Control plane/storage | Restore independently verified |
| FC-029 | Synchronisation | Reconcile authorized installations | Authorized admin | Enable, pair, retry | Stable IDs, events, checkpoint | Acknowledged projection | Opt-in; conflict policy; no replay | Conflict or outage | Sync Gateway/agent | Directed tests pass |
| FC-030 | M-Pesa | Accept mobile money | Cashier, manager | Initiate/check/confirm tender | Phone, amount, reference | Tender status | Official integration and confirmation rules | Timeout, mismatch | Kenya payment adapter | No duplicate charge or sale |
| FC-031 | KRA eTIMS | Submit fiscal records | Owner, accountant | Configure/submit/reconcile | KRA-verified payload | Receipt control response | Legal contract not assumed | Validation/rejection/service outage | Kenya fiscal adapter | Official sandbox and production verification |
| FC-032 | Universal search | Discover destinations | Authorized users | Search or invoke create action | Search text | Route/action result | Label aliases, permissions, exact route | Restricted destination | Navigation registry | Restricted discovery test passes |
| FC-033 | Platform tenant management | Administer businesses | Platform admin | Provision/suspend/reactivate | Business details | Tenant lifecycle | Separate control database | Invalid status transition | Control plane | Lifecycle and isolation pass |
| FC-034 | Licensing | Entitle local installations | Owner, platform admin | Activate/renew/revoke/transfer | Device and entitlement | Signed entitlement | Offline grace and audit | Clock/service failure | Licence service | Core POS remains permitted locally |
| FC-035 | Subscription billing | Bill Cloud businesses | Platform admin | Plan, invoice, pay, grace, suspend | Plan, payment, schedule | Subscription status | Separate from customer tender ledger | Payment failure | Billing provider/control plane | Authorized lifecycle test passes |
| FC-036 | Support | Diagnose authorized issue | Support technician | Open case, obtain grant | Ticket, scope | Diagnostic record | Least privilege and consent | Grant expired | Support service | No ungranted business access |
| FC-037 | Audit log | Reconstruct activity | Manager, auditor | Search and export | Time, actor, action | Audit event | Append-only view and tamper evidence | Restricted access | Audit storage | Actor/action/time/target present |
